Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 17, 2026  ·  Last updated: April 17, 2026

Short version: Late Talker stores everything on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. No account is required to use the app.

This Privacy Policy describes how Francisco Andrade ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the Late Talker: Speech Exercises mobile application (the "App"), available on iOS and Android.

By using the App you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Who uses this App

Late Talker is designed for use by parents and caregivers of children ages 1–4. The App is not intended to be used directly by children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The information you enter about your child (name, age) is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party.

2. Information we collect — and don't collect

Information stored locally on your device:

  • Your child's first name (optional — used only to personalise the home screen)
  • Your child's age in years
  • The learning track and stage your child is on
  • Session history and outcome logs (e.g. "Independent", "With help", "Not yet")
  • App appearance preference (light / dark / system)

All of the above is stored exclusively on your device using standard on-device storage (Android DataStore / iOS equivalent). None of this information is transmitted to our servers or to any third party.

Information we do not collect:

  • Email address or any contact details
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Audio, camera, or microphone data
  • Payment information (the App is currently free)

3. How we use information

The information stored on your device is used solely to personalise and operate the App for you — to display your child's name, remember where you are in the curriculum, and track session history so the App can suggest the right exercises each day.

We do not use any information for advertising, profiling, or analytics.

4. Third-party services

The App does not currently integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection SDKs. The App does not make network requests that transmit personal data.

If we add third-party services in the future (for example, subscription management or crash reporting), we will update this policy and disclose those services here before they are included in a published version of the App.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share any personal information — because we do not collect any personal information off your device.

6. Data retention and deletion

All data is stored on your device and is under your control at all times.

  • To clear your data: Uninstall the App. All locally stored data is removed when you uninstall.
  • To reset your profile within the App: Use the Settings screen to update your child's name, age, or learning path at any time.

Because we do not receive or store your data on any server, we have no data to delete on our end.

7. Children's privacy (COPPA)

The App is operated by and for parents and caregivers — it is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. The child information you enter (name, age) is stored only on your device and never transmitted to us.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child under 13, please contact us at the address below and we will take prompt steps to address it.

8. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California, you may have rights regarding your personal data including the right to access, correct, and delete it.

Because all data is stored locally on your device and we do not receive or process it, you exercise these rights directly by managing or uninstalling the App. If you have questions, contact us at the address below.

9. Security

Data is stored using your device's standard on-device storage mechanisms. We do not transmit data over the network, which means there is no server-side data at risk of a breach. The security of your device is the primary protection for the information stored by the App.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where material changes are made, notify you within the App. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
